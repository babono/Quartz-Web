<p>Quartz requires <strong>iOS 17 or later</strong>.</p>
<h3>The shield isn&#39;t blocking my apps</h3>
<p>Quartz blocks apps through Apple&#39;s Screen Time (Family Controls) framework, and that needs your permission to work. Check both of these:</p>
<ul>
<li>Open <strong>Settings → Screen Time</strong> and confirm Screen Time is switched on for your device.</li>
<li>Open Quartz and make sure it still has Screen Time access. If access was never granted or was later revoked, the shield cannot be applied.</li>
</ul>
<p>Also confirm the app you expected to be blocked is actually in your selection. Quartz only shields the apps and categories you picked — anything outside that list stays open.</p>
<h3>My apps are still blocked even though I have quota left</h3>
<p>The shield lifts when you open the app through Quartz and spend quota. Opening a shielded app directly will show the shield first; choose <strong>Open App &amp; Use Quota</strong> to spend from your remaining balance.</p>
<h3>My quota didn&#39;t reset</h3>
<p>Your quota resets once per day. Quartz performs that reset when the day rolls over — either at midnight or the next time you open the app after midnight. If you have not opened Quartz in a while, open it once and the day will settle.</p>
<h3>I lost all my Quartz</h3>
<p>The most likely cause is <strong>Emergency Unpause</strong>. It lifts the shields for the rest of the day, but it sets your Quartz balance to zero as the trade-off, and because the rest of that day is no longer monitored, the day does not count toward your streak. This is deliberate, and the balance cannot be restored.</p>
<h3>My streak reset</h3>
<p>A day only counts toward your streak if monitoring was active for it and no day in between was skipped. Turning monitoring off, using Emergency Unpause, or leaving a gap of one or more days will break the streak. Changing difficulty mode also resets it.</p>
<h3>The leaderboard is empty or out of date</h3>
<p>The leaderboard lives in Apple&#39;s iCloud. Make sure you are signed in to iCloud on your device and have a working connection, then reopen Quartz. Entries sync in the background, so a new balance can take a moment to appear for other people.</p>
<h3>How do I delete my account and my data?</h3>
<p>You can do this yourself from inside the app, in <strong>Profile → Delete Account</strong>. It removes your leaderboard entry and the data associated with your account. If you would rather we did it, email <strong><a href="mailto:babono@me.com">babono@me.com</a></strong>.</p>
<p>Your Screen Time data — which apps you limit and how long you use them — never leaves your device in the first place, so there is nothing on our side to delete.</p>
